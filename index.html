THE COMPANY

We build machines, not structures.

Matcheck started in 1992 building a weighing machine. Today the workshop builds machines and machine components: machined parts, welded frames and bases, and complete assemblies.

Bring us the job that has to be right first time, with a tight tolerance, a thick inspection pack and a client who reads every line of it. That is a normal day here.

Don't take our word for it. Come and stand on the shop floor, look at the work, and decide for yourself.

WHO WE BUILD FOR

Fired by your last fabricator? Welcome home.

Some workshops call it fussy. We call it a proper specification. If you mark up every drawing, ask for the inspection report before the invoice and send back parts that are a hair out, we will get on well.

The drawing is the boss

Tolerances, finishes and notes are built as drawn. If something on the drawing is unclear, we ask before we cut.

Inspection, then delivery

Parts are checked against the drawing before they leave the floor. Extended inspection and bespoke reporting on request.

Traceable to the person

Every weld traces to a coded welder. Every material carries its certificate from raw stock to finished part.

Open to any audit

Marketing is easy. A shop floor is harder to fake. Book a visit, audit the work and decide on what you see.
